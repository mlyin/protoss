# Swipe file

Our own, see `brand/copy.md` (headlines, sub-lines, about) and `brand/x-thread.md` (launch thread with its anti-slop audit).

Headline direction that works for us: a limit stated as a sentence a person would say.
- "No agent trades past the line you drew."
- "Your friends. One wall."
- "Pool $100. Watch every fill."

Niche patterns worth stealing the *shape* of (not the words): founder-voice build logs with one screenshot and one
number per post; "what if I had ..." backtest screenshots; a public PnL with the drawdown shown, not hidden.
Patterns to avoid: "we're excited to announce", agent-with-glowing-brain art, 20-tweet threads with a hook and no numbers.

Add entries as dated lines with a link and one sentence on why it worked.
