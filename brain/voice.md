# Voice

Matthew writes like a quant texting a friend: lowercase ok, short, concrete, a little blunt, zero hype.
First person singular for X and DMs. Product copy is plain declarative sentences.

## Banned (any one means rewrite)
excited to announce, thrilled, game-changer, revolutionize, unlock, empower, seamless, journey, dive in,
delve, landscape, in today's, the future of, supercharge, effortless, elevate, AI-powered, "it's not X it's Y",
"isn't just", emoji, hashtags, rocket/fire glyphs, em dashes (use periods or commas), exclamation marks in UI,
"genuinely", "honestly", triads of adjectives, rhetorical-question hooks, any claim of returns or "guaranteed".

## Always
- One concrete noun or number per sentence: a market, a dollar amount, a limit, a fill, a date.
- Say what it is before why it matters. Headlines could not fit another product.
- Sentence case. No trailing periods on labels and buttons. Numbers in one format, tabular.
- Status honesty: testnet / paper now; say what is unproven.

## Examples
- Good: "Lee's phalanx: $1,300 pooled, 4 people, 3x cap, stop at 12%. NVDA, SP500, BTC."
- Good: "No agent trades past the line you drew."
- Bad: "Unlock seamless AI-powered trading for your community."
