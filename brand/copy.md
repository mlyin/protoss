# Protoss brand

Name: Protoss. Site: protoss.trade. App: app.protoss.trade.

Headline: **Your friends. Your own trade.**

Description: Share a trading idea with friends. Choose your own amount, approve from your own wallet,
and keep your own position.

Short line: One idea. Separate accounts. Your wallet stays in control.

The mascot is a cartoon gold-and-cyan Protoss Probe. `probe.svg` is the editable native vector.
`web/public/probe.png` is the generated illustration used for the Telegram avatar and app header.
The animated states are idle, thinking, talking, working and success; conversation shows two Probes.
Thinking follows an active backtest request. Completion means a result arrived, not a profitable trade.

Use plain words: idea, group, amount, wallet, position, receipt. Name practice money and real money
clearly. Real orders need the user's selected authorization flow. Anthropic-linked perpetuals are
not Anthropic shares. Keep hypothetical performance separate from verified exchange fills.

Current assets: `web/public/favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`,
`icon-512.png`, `probe-social.png`, and `probe/*.svg`. The old `mark.svg`, `mark-light.svg`,
`mark-dark.svg`, and `hero.svg` filenames now resolve to Probe artwork for compatibility.
